# Wasatch Front / Tooele Valley Land Use Digest
**Period: June 2–17, 2026**

## This Week's Signal
Grantsville pushed the **Mack Canyon Subdivision** package through on June 3 — General Plan amendment, rezone from R-1-21 to R-1-12, and MDA all on the same agenda (parcels 01-065-0-0074 and 01-065-0-0014, near Mack Canyon Road and SR-138) — paired with a citywide land use code amendment loosening lot standards and street frontage. Two weeks later the Planning Commission queued up the **Orion Subdivision** rezone to RM-15 at 800 East Main. Grantsville is densifying its entitlement pipeline on both ends of town simultaneously.

## What Moved
- **Mack Canyon Subdivision (Grantsville)** — General Plan amendment + downzone to R-1-12 + MDA approved as a package on 6/3 near Mack Canyon Rd/SR-138. Smaller-lot single-family entitlement on the western edge of Grantsville.
- **Land Use Code §4.5 amendment (Grantsville)** — Lot standards and street frontage rewrite passed the same night as Mack Canyon. Worth pulling the redline; it likely greases the skids for future R-1-12 and PUD product.
- **Orion Subdivision (Grantsville)** — General Plan amendment + rezone from RR-1/RM-7 to **RM-15** at 800 East Main on 6/16. Four parcels. This is multifamily-density on Main Street, eastern gateway.
- **Alington Plat PUD (Grantsville)** — PUD in the Mixed-Use zone, 6/16 Planning Commission. Two-parcel assemblage (01-059-0-0040, -0039).
- **Sun Sage Terrace Phases 3–6 (Grantsville)** — MDA and lot layout review on 6/16. Multi-phase residential continuing to build out.
- **Erda East & West Disconnect Applications** — City Council took up both disconnect petitions on 6/11 with "tentative decision and direction to City Attorney." This is the live boundary fight; outcome dictates which jurisdiction entitles future development on the disconnecting parcels.
- **Erda FY26-27 Budget + Certified Tax Rate (Ord. 26-08)** — Adopted 6/11. First full-cycle budget signals are now public for the young city.
- **Tooele Planning Commission 6/24** — General Plan Map amendment at **1280 N / 200 E from Medium Density Residential to Light Industrial** (4.1 acres). Also CUP for a drive-through at **2400 N and SR-36**. Both are SR-36 corridor signals.
- **Tooele Code 7-16a-3 (Residential Special Districts)** — Minimum land requirements amended 6/3; companion amendment on Commercial Special Districts contiguity heard 6/24. The city is recalibrating its overlay/master-planned thresholds.

## Pattern Watch
1. **Grantsville is rezoning up and loosening standards in the same cycle.** Mack Canyon (downzone to smaller lots) + the §4.5 frontage/lot standards amendment + Orion's jump to RM-15 + Alington PUD all hit within 14 days. This is not one-off activity; the city is actively reshaping its entitlement framework. Anyone holding raw ground in Grantsville should reprice optionality.
2. **SR-138 / SR-36 corridors both surfacing.** Mack Canyon sits at Mack Canyon Rd/SR-138 (Grantsville west); Tooele's drive-through CUP and the 4.1-acre MDR→Light Industrial flip are both on the SR-36 corridor (Tooele north). Two separate jurisdictions, two highway corridors, same week.
3. **Erda's disconnect proceedings are the structural story.** Whichever way the East and West petitions resolve will redraw the entitlement map between Erda, Grantsville, and Tooele City. Until the Council issues final direction, treat all Erda-adjacent deals as jurisdictionally contingent.
