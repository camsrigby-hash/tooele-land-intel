# Wasatch Front / Tooele Valley Land Use Digest
**Period: May 19 – June 11, 2026**

## This Week's Signal
Grantsville advanced the full Mack Canyon Subdivision package on June 3 — General Plan amendment, rezone from R-1-21 to R-1-12, and Master Development Agreement (parcels 01-065-0-0074 and 01-065-0-0014) — paired with a Land Use Management Code amendment to Section 4.5 loosening lot standards and street frontage requirements. The simultaneous code change is the tell: Grantsville is reshaping its baseline residential standard to a smaller-lot product, not just approving one project.

## What Moved

- **Mack Canyon Subdivision (Grantsville)** — General Plan amendment + R-1-21 to R-1-12 downzone + MDA, all on the same June 3 agenda. Density nearly doubles per acre on the affected parcels.
- **Grantsville LUMC §4.5 amendment** — Citywide change to lot standards and street frontage rules, moving in lockstep with Mack Canyon. Worth reading as a precedent-setter for future Grantsville subdivisions.
- **Willow Fields Subdivision MDA (Grantsville)** — Master Development Agreement at Old Lincoln Highway and Champion Drive (May 22). Second active MDA on Grantsville's west/south edge alongside Mack Canyon.
- **Arbon Acres Preliminary Plat (Grantsville)** — 500 South and Hale Street (May 22). Adds to the Grantsville subdivision pipeline; acreage not disclosed in agenda.
- **Presidents Park Townhomes Preliminary Plat (Grantsville)** — Nygreen Street and Washington Avenue (May 19). Townhome product inside Grantsville city — notable given the historical SF-detached default.
- **Holiday Oil / Kenneth Fawson Subdivision amendment (Grantsville)** — 794 E Main Street (May 19). Commercial final plat amendment on the Main Street/SR-138 frontage.
- **Copper Cove Preliminary Plat Phase 1 (Erda)** — 45 residential lots, May 26 Planning Commission. First substantive Erda subdivision item on the calendar; phase count implies a longer pipeline behind it.
- **Grantsville Master Transportation Plan amendments (May 22)** — Quietly significant given the volume of subdivision activity feeding into it. Alignment changes here affect every project listed above.

## Pattern Watch

- **Grantsville is in a coordinated up-zoning posture.** The Mack Canyon downzone to R-1-12, the §4.5 code amendment, the Willow Fields MDA, Arbon Acres, and Presidents Park townhomes all hit within 14 days. This is not a one-off; it's a regulatory environment shift toward smaller lots and higher unit counts. Expect more R-1-21 parcels to seek R-1-12 over the next 6–12 months citing Mack Canyon as precedent.
- **Erda is finally producing tradeable signal.** Copper Cove's 45-lot Phase 1 is the first real subdivision activity to surface from Erda's planning commission in this dataset. Watch whether Phase 2+ filings follow quickly or stall — that will indicate whether Erda's young municipal apparatus can actually process pipeline.
- **The Grantsville Main Street / SR-138 corridor is layering commercial (Holiday Oil) on top of residential growth (Mack Canyon, Willow Fields, Arbon Acres) without a visible arterial capacity plan beyond the Master Transportation Plan amendments. Traffic/impact-fee exposure is rising.**
