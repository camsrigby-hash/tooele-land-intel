# Wasatch Front / Tooele Valley Land Use Digest
**Period: April 21 – May 14, 2026**

## This Week's Signal
Grantsville's 113-acre Wellstone (a.k.a. Wellington) Subdivision moved through both Planning Commission (4/21) and City Council (5/6) with a paired General Plan amendment and R-1-21 → R-1-12 rezone — the largest density-up move in Tooele Valley this period. Combined with Erda's Copper Cove 45-lot Phase 1 preliminary plat (4/28) and two Erda parcel disconnection petitions (5/14), the Tooele Valley residential pipeline is visibly thickening despite the broader slowdown narrative.

## What Moved

- **Wellstone/Wellington Subdivision (Grantsville)** — ~113 acres near Pear St & Quirk St; General Plan amendment + rezone R-1-21 to R-1-12. Note the name discrepancy between the 4/21 PC packet ("Wellstone") and 5/6 Council packet ("Wellington") on identical parcels — worth confirming which is operative.
- **Copper Cove Phase 1 (Erda)** — 45-lot residential preliminary plat at Planning Commission 4/28. First substantive subdivision activity since Erda incorporated; sets precedent for review standards.
- **Erda Disconnection Petitions (Erda)** — Two separate parcels petitioning to disconnect from Erda City, 5/14 Council. Disconnection this early in a new city's life is a signal — who's leaving, and to where (county? Grantsville annexation play?).
- **Spring Run Plaza (Eagle Mountain)** — Master Development Agreement + preliminary plat for 6 commercial lots on Spring Run Parkway, parcel 58:033:0628. Real commercial absorption in Eagle Mountain remains rare; track the tenant mix.
- **Cedar Hollow Holdings Annexation (Lehi)** — 15.31 acres at 400 S Center Street, Council 4/28. Center Street infill of this scale is unusual in Lehi.
- **Perry Commercial — Traverse Mountain Area Plan amendment (Lehi)** — Moves residential units from West Canyon to the Timpanogos Highway corridor. Density transfers along Timp Highway change underwriting on adjacent commercial pads.
- **Veranda West (Draper)** — Master Area Plan amendment + Chapter 9-14 zoning text amendment + CUP for outdoor recreation in TSD zone, all 5/14. Three coordinated actions on one project = developer is restructuring entitlements, not just tweaking.
- **620 South Annexation (American Fork)** — 7.03 acres at 6695 W 7300 N; PC 5/6, Council 5/26. West-side AF annexation in the SR-73/Pioneer Crossing influence zone.
- **Alternative Energy Overlay Zone (Eagle Mountain)** — New EMMC 17.64 AEOZ creation, PC 5/12. Data center / solar / battery storage framework is being built; precedes specific applications.
- **Brandon Park Rezone (Eagle Mountain)** — Future Land Use Map + Zoning Map amendments, Council 5/5 after PC 4/21 hearing.

## Pattern Watch

- **Tooele Valley residential entitlement is active, not dormant.** Wellstone (113 ac density-up), Copper Cove (45 lots), and Matthews Meadows Phase 2 extension request all hit in 14 days. If you're tracking lot supply in Grantsville/Erda, the pipeline is being refilled now — before any visible vertical activity.
- **Code-cleanup cycle across multiple cities.** Grantsville is amending Chapters 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 14, 15, 16, 20, and 21 simultaneously (5/6) plus separate Section 4.5 lot standards work; Tooele is reworking landscaping (7-14-11), parking (Title 7 Ch 4), and multi-family fencing (7-11a-17); Eagle Mountain is amending Chapter 16.15 Concept Plans and WUI setbacks; Saratoga Springs is amending Title 18 grading and Title 19 drive-thru rules. This is the volume of ordinance churn that produces accidental nonconformities — diligence on any pending deal should re-pull current code.
- **Drive-thru and auto-commercial re-regulation is a mini-trend.** Eagle Mountain (EMMC 17.72 drive-throughs/structured parking), Saratoga Springs (Title 19.09.11 drive-thru), Tooele (off-street parking for auto-related uses), and Spanish Fork (Doug Smith auto dealership signage, Maverik signage) all touched this category. Pad-site underwriting for QSR/auto in these cities should pause until rules settle.
