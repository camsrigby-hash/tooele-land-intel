# Wasatch Front / Tooele Valley Land Use Digest
**Period: June 16 – July 9, 2026**

## This Week's Signal
Grantsville is the story. The **Origin Subdivision** rezone at 800 East Main Street (five parcels, RR-1/RM-7 → **RM-15**) plus the **Alington PUD** at 948 East/SR-138 and master development agreements for **Sun Sage Terrace (Phases 3-6)**, **Willow Fields**, and **Falcons Landing** put at least five active large-format residential projects on the Planning Commission docket in a single 21-day window. If you have raw ground east of Main or along SR-138, this is your repricing event.

## What Moved

- **Origin Subdivision (Grantsville)** — 800 East Main Street, 5 parcels, General Plan amendment + rezone to **RM-15** (multifamily). Highest-density ask in the Grantsville stack; sets the ceiling for east-side density discussions.
- **Alington PUD (Grantsville)** — 948 East and SR-138, parcels 01-059-0-0040/0039. Both PUD and MDA advanced from 6/16 to 7/07 PC. Second high-density node clustering on the east approach.
- **Sun Sage Terrace Phases 3-6 (Grantsville)** — MDA and lot layout heard 6/16. Phasing through Phase 6 implies a multi-year absorption commitment the city is now locking in.
- **Falcons Landing (Grantsville)** — MDA at Booth Street and Wild Rose Drive, 7/07. Another new MDA stacked on the same agenda as Alington.
- **Willow Fields (Grantsville)** — MDA at Old Lincoln Highway and Champion Drive, 6/17 Council. Different geography than the SR-138/Main cluster — worth tracking separately.
- **Tooele 4.1-acre Land Use Map Amendment** — 1280 North and 200 East, **Medium Density Residential → Light Industrial**. Industrial conversion of residentially-designated land north of town; unusual direction of travel.
- **Tooele CUP — Accessory Drive-Through at 2400 N and SR-36** — North-end SR-36 commercial activity continues to push outward.
- **Erda Disconnect Petitions Denied (Ord. 26-09, 26-10)** — Council denied both the East Disconnect and West Disconnect petitions on 6/25. Erda's boundaries are now settled for the near term; properties stay in city jurisdiction.

## Pattern Watch

- **SR-138/East Main Grantsville corridor is heating up.** Origin (RM-15 ask), Alington PUD, Holiday Oil commercial at 794 E Main, and Kenneth Fawson subdivision amendment all sit within roughly a mile of each other. This is no longer one-off activity — it's a corridor.
- **Grantsville MDA volume is abnormal.** Three master development agreements (Sun Sage 3-6, Willow Fields, Falcons Landing) plus an Alington MDA in 14 days. Staff bandwidth and infrastructure commitments are being spent quickly; expect a slowdown or impact-fee conversation next.
- **Erda has chosen to stay whole.** Twin disconnect denials remove a speculative overhang on parcels that petitioners likely wanted out from under Erda's land use regime. Worth knowing who filed and why.
- **Sign code amendments everywhere** (Eagle Mountain, Lehi, Tooele) — political signage cycle ahead of 2026 elections, not a land-use signal.
