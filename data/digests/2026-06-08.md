# Wasatch Front + Tooele Valley Land Use Digest
**Period: May 26 – June 16, 2026**

## This Week's Signal
Grantsville pushed the **Mack Canyon Subdivision** package across the finish line on June 3 — General Plan amendment, rezone from R-1-21 to R-1-12 (denser), and a Master Development Agreement, all on parcels 01-065-0-0074 and 01-065-0-0014 near Mack Canyon Road and SR-138. Paired with a same-night amendment to LUMC Section 4.5 (lot standards and street frontage), Grantsville is actively loosening lot-size constraints while approving denser product on the SR-138 corridor. Two weeks later (June 16), the Planning Commission queued up **Orion Subdivision** at 800 East Main — a rezone request from RR-1/RM-7 to **RM-15** — signaling that multi-family density is now in play inside Grantsville's core, not just on the edges.

## What Moved
- **Mack Canyon Subdivision (Grantsville)** — GP amendment, rezone R-1-21 → R-1-12, and MDA all approved same night (Ord. 2026-22/23/24). Two-parcel assemblage near Mack Canyon Rd / SR-138. The rezone roughly doubles allowable density.
- **LUMC Section 4.5 Amendment (Grantsville, Ord. 2026-25)** — Lot standards and street frontage rules rewritten the same evening as Mack Canyon. Worth pulling the redline; this is the code change that makes future R-1-12 projects easier.
- **Orion Subdivision (Grantsville, 800 E Main)** — GP amendment + rezone from RR-1/RM-7 to **RM-15** on four parcels (01-059-0-0041, -0001, -0028, -0027). RM-15 is the city's densest residential category. First read June 16.
- **Alington Plat PUD (Grantsville, 948 East & SR-138)** — PUD on parcels 01-059-0-0040/-0039, June 16 PC. Second SR-138-adjacent project moving this period.
- **Sun Sage Terrace Phases 3–6 (Grantsville)** — MDA and lot layout for four additional phases before Planning Commission June 16. Indicates project is scaling, not stalling.
- **Scenic Mountain Area Rezoning (Eagle Mountain)** — Mass rezone of **223 properties** before Council June 2. Scale alone makes this worth a deep look; pull the staff report.
- **Eagle Mountain Annexation Policy Plan** — Identifies **seven unincorporated Utah County areas** as annexation targets. PC reviewed May 26 and June 9; Council June 2. A future-land-use map amendment is now tied to it.
- **Horizon MDP (Eagle Mountain)** — 110 SFD lots on ~109 acres (parcel 58:040:0378), Council June 2. ~1.0 du/ac — low density for EM, suggests topography or water constraints.
- **Bishop Development Agreement (Lehi, 2424 W 900 N)** — Density bump from existing approval to **13 lots on 7 acres**. Small, but indicative of Lehi PC entertaining mid-project intensification.
- **Walker Zone Change (Lehi, 915 W State St)** — 2.58 acres from R-1-8 to **C-H** (Highway Commercial). Conversion of residential to commercial frontage on State Street.
- **Veranda West Master Area Plan Amendment (Draper, 13708 S 600 W)** — MAP amendment plus a related zoning text amendment. MAP amendments in Draper are rare and usually meaningful — get the application.
- **Herriman Development Agreement** — **17.95-acre mixed-use** at 3700 W 15000 S, Council May 27. Sizeable mixed-use in the SW quadrant; details not in agenda summary.
- **Bluffdale GP Amendment + Rezone (13942 S 2700 W)** — Very Low Density Residential → **Commercial**, with a paired Development Agreement for a Flex Retail project at 2700 W / 2950 W on June 24. Flex retail in a VLDR area is a meaningful conversion.
- **Erda — Copper Cove Preliminary Plat Phase 1** — 45-lot residential subdivision before PC May 26. Erda's PC subsequently canceled its June 9 meeting, so action paused.

## Pattern Watch
1. **SR-138 corridor in Grantsville is heating up.** Mack Canyon (Mack Canyon Rd/SR-138), Alington PUD (948 E & SR-138), and Orion (800 E Main, one block off) are all moving in a 14-day window. Combined with the LUMC 4.5 rewrite and an RM-15 rezone request, Grantsville is signaling it will accept denser product along and just off SR-138. If you're sitting on raw ground in this band, the comps and entitlements environment just shifted in your favor.
2. **Density-up rezones are the dominant request type this period** across jurisdictions: Mack Canyon (R-1-21 → R-1-12), Orion (RR-1/RM-7 → RM-15), Bishop (more lots on same acreage), Bluffdale (VLDR → Commercial), Lehi Walker (R-1-8 → C-H), The Reserve (South Jordan). Almost no down-zonings. Cities are absorbing growth pressure through code, not just new greenfield.
3. **Eagle Mountain is positioning for expansion on two fronts** — the Annexation Policy Plan (7 target areas) and the 223-parcel Scenic Mountain rezone are moving in parallel. This is a coordinated land-strategy moment, not routine housekeeping.

## Diligence Queue
1. **Grantsville — pull Ord. 2026-25 (LUMC Sec. 4.5 redline).** What exactly changed on lot standards and frontage? This is the lever that determines yield on every future Grantsville rezone, including Orion.
2. **Grantsville — call zoning re: Orion Subdivision (800 E Main).** Who's the applicant, what's the unit count proposed under RM-15, and is there a concurrent MDA in the works? RM-15 inside city limits is a notable jump.

4. **Eagle Mountain — map the 7 annexation target areas** against current Utah County ownership records. The Future Land Use Map amendment tied to the Annexation Policy Plan will telegraph where EM expects growth next.
5. **Bluffdale — request the Flex Retail development agreement for 2700 W / 2950 W** before the June 24 hearing. A VLDR-to-Commercial conversion paired with a same-day DA suggests a known tenant or user; identify it.
6. **Herriman — pull the 17.95-acre mixed-use DA at 3700 W 15000 S.** No detail in the agenda; this is the largest single mixed-use item in the period and deserves a full read.
