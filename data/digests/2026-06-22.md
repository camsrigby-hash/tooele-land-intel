# Wasatch Front / Tooele Valley Land Use Digest
**Period: June 9–23, 2026**

## This Week's Signal
Grantsville is the story this period: the Origin Subdivision rezone at 800 East Main Street (five parcels, RR-1/RM-7 to RM-15) advanced alongside the Alington PUD at 948 East/SR-138 and the Sun Sage Terrace Phases 3-6 MDA — three substantial residential entitlement moves on the east side of town heard the same night. Meanwhile, Erda's City Council took up both East and West Disconnect Applications on June 11, which could materially redraw the city's boundary and tax base.

## What Moved
- **Origin Subdivision (Grantsville)** — 800 East Main Street, 5 parcels, General Plan amendment + rezone from RR-1/RM-7 to **RM-15**. The density jump to 15 units/acre on a Main Street frontage is the most aggressive residential upzone in Tooele Valley this cycle.
- **Alington Plat PUD (Grantsville)** — 948 East and SR-138, two parcels (01-059-0-0040/0039). PUD on the SR-138 corridor adjacent to the Origin parcels — same node, same night.
- **Sun Sage Terrace Phases 3-6 (Grantsville)** — Master Development Agreement and lot layout heard June 16. Multi-phase pipeline now locking in lot configuration.
- **Willow Fields Subdivision MDA (Grantsville)** — Old Lincoln Highway and Champion Drive. New MDA on the west/north edge of town — different corridor than the Main Street activity.
- **Erda East & West Disconnect Applications** — Council took tentative decisions and directed the City Attorney on June 11. Disconnects, not annexations — territory potentially leaving Erda. Direction to counsel suggests this is moving past discussion.
- **Kuwahara Nursery (Draper)** — Land Use Map + Zoning Map amendment and Development Agreement for **21 single-family dwellings**. Nursery-to-residential conversion at Planning Commission June 25.
- **Rivian Draper (Draper)** — Site plan and deviation requests for new automobile dealership at 11601 S Lone Peak Pkwy. First Rivian-branded dealership site plan in the south valley.
- **Tooele Light Industrial Map Amendment** — 4.1 acres at 1280 North and 200 East, **Medium Density Residential to Light Industrial**. Residential-to-industrial flips are rare; worth understanding what's driving it.
- **Tooele CUP — Accessory Drive-Through at 2400 N / SR-36** — Pad-level activity on the north SR-36 commercial frontage.
- **Bluffdale GPA + Rezone at 13942 S 2700 W** — Very Low Density Residential to **Commercial**, paired with a Development Agreement for four flex retail buildings on 3.914 acres at 2700 W / 2950 W. Same applicant footprint, same meeting.
- **Walker Zone Change (Lehi)** — 915 West State Street, 2.58 acres, R-1-8 to **C-H** (Highway Commercial). State Street frontage flipping out of residential.
- **Holiday Oil at 794 E Main Street (Grantsville)** — Kenneth Fawson Subdivision amendment + Holiday Oil commercial final plat. Another Main Street/east side commercial node activating.

## Pattern Watch
1. **Grantsville's east Main Street / SR-138 node is consolidating.** Origin (800 E Main), Alington (948 E / SR-138), and the Holiday Oil/Fawson plat (794 E Main) are all within a few blocks of each other and all moved the same week. Whoever is assembling or building speculatively in that node has a window to act before the entitlements harden values.
2. **State highway commercial conversions are clustering.** Bluffdale (2700 W to commercial with flex retail), Lehi Walker (State Street to C-H), Tooele (SR-36 drive-through CUP), and Grantsville's SR-138 PUD all point to highway-frontage residential or low-density parcels being repriced as commercial in the same two-week window.
3. **Erda disconnects are a standalone signal.** Unlike the rest of the region's growth-mode activity, Erda is processing territory leaving the city. If approved, this changes the buyer/seller calculus on any parcel near the disconnect boundaries — both for what stays in Erda and what reverts to county jurisdiction.
