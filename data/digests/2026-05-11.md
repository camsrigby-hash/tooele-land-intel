# Wasatch Front + Tooele Valley Land Use Digest
**Period: April 28 – May 12, 2026**

## This Week's Signal
Two disconnection petitions filed against Erda City on May 14 are the headline — landowners are actively trying to exit Erda's corporate limits, which signals real friction between the new city and its land base. Meanwhile, Grantsville pushed a heavy land-use docket through May 6, including the Wellstone/Wellington rezone (~113 acres near Pear & Quirk from R-1-21 to R-1-12) and sweeping code amendments to Chapters 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 14, 15, 16, 20, and 21 in a single action.

## What Moved

- **Erda — Disconnection Petitions (x2), Erda City Council, May 14.** Two separate parcels petitioning to be removed from Erda corporate limits. Petitioner names and acreage not in agenda excerpts. This is the item to chase first — disconnection is rare and usually signals a tax, zoning, or service dispute.
- **Erda — Copper Cove Preliminary Plat Phase 1, Planning Commission, April 28.** 45-lot residential subdivision. First substantial new subdivision under Erda's own PC review since incorporation matured.
- **Grantsville — Wellstone/Wellington Subdivision, City Council, May 6.** General Plan amendment + rezone from R-1-21 to R-1-12 across four parcels (01-071-0-0012, 01-074-0-0012, 01-074-0-0010, 11-010-0-0002) near Pear St & Quirk St. Density upzone on ~113 acres. Note: agenda lists both "Wellstone" and "Wellington" — verify which is correct.
- **Grantsville — 519 W Main Street rezone, City Council, May 8.** A-10 (Agricultural) to C-S (Shopping Commercial). Highway-frontage ag-to-commercial conversion on Main.
- **Grantsville — 20-Year Annexation Policy Plan, City Council, May 8.** Framework adoption. Sets the legal envelope for future Grantsville growth boundaries — directly relevant to anyone holding unincorporated Tooele Valley dirt.
- **Grantsville — Comprehensive Land Use Code rewrite, City Council, May 6 & 8.** Amendments to Chapters 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 14, 15, 16, 20, 21 plus separate amendments to definitions, development standards, and Section 4.5 (lot standards/street frontage). This is a near-total code overhaul moving in parallel tracks.
- **Grantsville — Presidents Park Townhomes Preliminary Plat, Planning Commission, May 19.** Townhome project at Nygreen St & Washington Ave. Attached product inside Grantsville is still uncommon enough to flag.
- **Grantsville — Holiday Oil / Kenneth Fawson Subdivision amendment, Planning Commission, May 19.** Commercial Final Plat at 794 E Main Street. Another Main Street commercial node activating.

## Pattern Watch

- **Grantsville is rewriting its rulebook while simultaneously approving entitlements under it.** The Chapter 4–21 code amendments, the Section 4.5 lot/frontage amendment, the annexation policy plan, and the impact fee analysis amendments are all moving inside a two-week window — alongside the Wellstone upzone and the 519 W Main commercial rezone. Any deal underwritten to current Grantsville standards needs a fresh read of the post-amendment code before close.
- **Tooele Valley density-up signal.** Wellstone (R-1-21 → R-1-12) in Grantsville plus Copper Cove's 45-lot Phase 1 in Erda point to acceptance of smaller lots than the historical valley pattern. Worth testing whether comparable parcels nearby can support the same rezone argument.
- **Main Street Grantsville is the commercial corridor right now.** 519 W Main (rezone to C-S), 794 E Main (Holiday Oil), and the 454 E Main "Le Property" mixed-use concept all surfaced this period. Three active commercial moves on a single corridor inside two weeks is not coincidence.
